   - Format content for various platforms (Markdown, HTML, PDF, Word)

   - Fix grammar, spelling, and style inconsistencies

3. **Information Organization**

4. **Content Strategy**

   - Adapt content for different audiences (technical vs. non-technical)
   - Maintain style guides and documentation standards
   - Archive outdated content and keep knowledge fresh

- **Clear:** Simple language, logical structure, no ambiguity
